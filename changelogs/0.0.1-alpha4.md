# Changelog of 0.0.1-alpha4

Fourth alpha of the 0.0.1 line. Covers everything since `0.0.1-alpha3`.

## Added

### Gameplay

- Worn armour absorbs damage and wears out as it takes hits.
- Hunger drains with movement and actions, food refills it, and an empty
  bar starves the player.
- Experience levels and orbs, plus an `/xp` command.
- Enchantments on a held item now take effect.
- Lava as a fluid, including what happens when it meets water.
- Furnaces smelt, burn fuel and show their progress.
- Barrels, ender chests and shulker boxes open.
- Beds set a personal spawn point; players respawn there and the client is
  told where it is.
- Scoreboard objectives, display slots and teams.

### Worlds

- Players come back in the world they logged out of, and each world keeps
  its players' spawn points.
- Per world seeds that change every builtin generator's terrain, seeded
  spawns and a `/seed` command. Worlds from before seeds keep working.
- Cave carving in the overworld generator: winding spaghetti tunnels and
  wider cheese chambers.
- Vanilla subchunks are written as well as read.

### Networking and security

- NetherNet offers have their identity token checked against Microsoft's
  signing keys, and the login chain is bound to the key that opened the
  transport.
- Authenticated logins over an anonymous NetherNet connection are refused.
- New `[network]` settings: `require_identity`, `verify_identity_token`,
  `ice_servers`, `advertise_addresses`, `media_port_min`/`media_port_max`,
  `identity_file` and `identity_domain`.
- Resource packs with encrypted content are served, and a pack that can't
  be inspected fails instead of being assumed to be plaintext.

### Server

- The world actor boundary is hardened and instrumented, and `World.exec`
  transactions are labelled in world metrics.

## Changed

- Event handlers live on the player or world whose state changes. The old
  bus dispatched to a per world instance nothing could register on, so
  events like block place, player hurt, death and move never reached a
  handler.
- `Player` has its own verbs (messaging, inventory, damage, kill, gamemode,
  disconnect, status effects), reached through a world transaction.
- The world actor and the chunk service moved into their own `worldrt`
  module, and the connection moved out of the session into `Conn`.
- Viewers get a semantic vocabulary and per viewer actor ids, replacing the
  viewer packet pipe.
- World storage keeps block data as columns and every block entity as one
  record. Existing worlds are migrated when they are opened.
- Moved to protocol `v1.4.0` (single version, 1.26.50 / 2192).
- CI pins V `d1df2de` and builds without the module cache.
- The project is relicensed to MIT.

## Fixed

- Block state hashes, palette ids and other 32 bit values read through
  `int` lost their sign now that V's `int` is 64 bit, so the client could
  be sent the wrong block runtime ids.
- The player identity is taken only from the token Mojang's chain vouches
  for.
- A damaged player save is no longer treated as a new player.
- Client and login chain keys are read without `crypto.ecdsa`'s PEM parser.
- The state upgrader changed the caller's block properties in place.
- Untranslated actor ids in viewer updates.
- Queued task times are paired with the tasks that were actually submitted.

## Upgrading

Run `v install` from the repository root to pick up protocol `v1.4.0`. A
leftover protocol `v2.0.x` copy in `~/.vmodules/bedrock_v/protocol` will not
build. Use V `d1df2de` or newer, older V builds hit compiler bugs this
release works around.
